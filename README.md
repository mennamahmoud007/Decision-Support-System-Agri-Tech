🌱 Sand2Seed
From Soil Data to Smart Farming Decisions
📌 Overview

Sand2Seed is a Decision Support System (DSS) designed to help farmers and agricultural stakeholders make data-driven farming decisions based on soil and climate conditions.

Instead of relying on trial-and-error farming, Sand2Seed analyzes environmental and soil indicators and translates them into clear, actionable insights that support:

Sustainable farming

Resource efficiency

Risk reduction

Long-term soil health

🚨 Problem Statement

Agricultural decisions — especially in reclaimed or arid lands — are often made without proper environmental analysis.

This leads to:

Water overuse

Soil degradation

Crop failure

Nutrient imbalance

Reduced long-term productivity

There is a need for a system that transforms raw environmental data into intelligent farming decisions.

💡 Our Solution

Sand2Seed is a web-based decision support system that:

Takes soil & climate inputs

Evaluates crop suitability

Detects environmental stress

Calculates soil health indicators

Provides sustainability-focused insights

The goal is not only to maximize yield — but to ensure sustainable agricultural practices.

⚙️ How It Works

The system processes:

🌡 Temperature

💧 Rainfall

💦 Humidity

🧪 N, P, K Levels

🧂 Soil pH

🌾 Crop Type

It then generates:

Climate Suitability Index

Water Stress Level

Soil Health Score

Nutrient Deficiency Detection

Risk Alerts
