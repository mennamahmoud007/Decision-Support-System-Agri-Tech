Best Practices

Sustainability Tips

Practical guidance for farmers working in arid and desert environments.

💧

Irrigation

Use drip irrigation instead of flooding. Irrigate early morning or after sunset to reduce evaporation. Avoid over-irrigation — excess water damages soil structure. If water is limited, add organic matter to improve retention.

🌱

Soil Care

Add compost or organic fertilizer before planting. Avoid excessive chemical fertilizers and deep plowing. Sandy soil needs extra organic matter and frequent small-amount irrigation.

🌿

Crop Selection

Choose heat- and drought-tolerant crops. If water is limited, prefer Barley, Sorghum, Sesame, or Date Palm. Avoid high-water-demand crops in desert conditions.

☀️

Temperature & Climate

During extreme heat, avoid irrigation at noon. Use mulch (straw or plant residues) on the soil surface. Yellow leaves indicate nutrient deficiency; wilting indicates improper irrigation.

🐜

Desert Ecosystem

Preserve native desert plants around the field. Do not remove all natural vegetation or plant too close to sand dunes. Minimize chemical pesticide and fertilizer use.

♻️

Sustainability

Save water to keep the land productive long-term. Sustainable farming improves yield and protects nature. Poor decisions cause long-term land degradation.

📋 Daily Worker Checklist

  • Check plant condition
  • Irrigate as recommended
  • Do not overuse fertilizers
  • Protect native desert plants
  • Report any issues immediately
